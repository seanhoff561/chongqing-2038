# Playtest

What only a person can judge: feel, fun, the real frame rate. Play the desktop game (it updates itself on start) and
reply in chat with what you saw; a line per item is plenty.

## Full-run checklist

Start a new run from the main menu and play it through to an ending, or as far as you get. For each, say how it felt
and anything wrong.

1. **The opening.** You wake in the hideout's blue light; Anna's three lines, then the laptop and the bag (E). Does it
   read clearly, and is the hand-off to control obvious?
2. **The laptop.** Tab, then Q to sit. Anna's line on each app the first time; the map filling in as you learn places;
   the Training Room. Any hitch opening it?
3. **Getting out.** The alley, the drainpipe to the roofs, the streets. Leaves under the trees, the alley's neon (dark
   by day, ice blue at night), the ground solid everywhere?
4. **Machines.** How soon you're seen upright by day and crouched at night; how each kind attacks; explosions and
   knockback. Do escapes feel fair or too hard (D-148)?
5. **Living.** A few days: food, water, sleep, a shelter with a bed. Too easy, too hard?
6. **Your sister.** Her notes from the family flat to her exit. Do they lead you without giving it away?
7. **The places.** The secure sites, the shelter and metro, the cableway, Hongyadong, Raffles City and The Crystal,
   the border wall. Which felt best, which felt thin?
8. **An escape.** Any route: the bridge's rail deck, the cabin or trolley, the boat at night, the flooded tunnel. With
   Xiaoyu or without. Does the ending land?
9. **Death.** Die once on purpose. Waking from the save, and a new run after an ending.
10. **Frame rate.** Your fps at the substation, on the rail deck, in the cabin, on the river past Chaotianmen, in the
    metro and the flooded extension, by day and night; and how long the city takes to load (D-147).
11. **Your own songs** in the laptop's Songs app, and the city's music going quiet while they play.
